How to play

On a keyboard
Walk
W S or ↑ ↓ to walk up and down the path. Hold Shift to hurry.
Step
A D or ← → to move across the path.
Jump
Space. Jump while stepping toward the edge to leap off it and land on the slope below.
Look
Drag with the mouse to look around. Scroll to zoom in and out.
Lanterns
Walk up to one and press E or Enter, or click its sign.
Scenes
Click, drag or swipe as the bubble says; when a scene asks you to hold, hold Space; where you walk, W or ↑ walks too. A D turn your view. Number keys 1–9 also choose things in order. Esc leaves.
Views
J opens the journal. V switches to bird's-eye view; drag to turn it and scroll to zoom.
On a phone or tablet
Walk
Put a finger anywhere and drag: up to walk on, down to walk back, left or right to step across the path.
Jump
Tap. While dragging, tap with a second finger. Jump toward the edge to leap off it.
Lanterns
Walk up to one and tap its sign.
Scenes
Tap, drag or swipe as the bubble says; when a scene asks you to hold, press and hold.
Views
The journal and bird's-eye view are at the top. In bird's-eye view, drag to turn and pinch to zoom. On a phone, two fingers move the camera anywhere: pinch to zoom, slide both to turn and tilt.
Lanterns
Each lantern on the path is a chapter. Open it to play a short scene.
Feature lanterns
The taller lanterns with a cross on top are longer scenes, each built around one of John's great images.
Teaching
Each thing you do shows one line of the chapter. Finishing the scene shows the chapter in a sentence.
Arches
An arch stands at the start of each stage of the climb, named as on the line at the left: the active nights of sense and spirit, the passive nights of sense and spirit, and the Living Flame. Just walk through.
Journal
Every section of the climb, with how many lanterns you have lit. Jump to any part of the mountain from there.

The explanations are summaries in plain words, written for study. They do not replace the text of St John of the Cross.
